Our Mission

Closing the opportunity gap, one student at a time.

Too many bright students fall behind not because of ability, but because of access — to a quiet place to study, a notebook and pencil, or someone who believes in them. EMI exists to change that.

We connect students from underserved communities with volunteer tutors, put essential learning supplies in their hands, and build the confidence they need to succeed in school and beyond.

What We Do

Our Programs

📚

Free Tutoring

One-on-one and small-group sessions in math, reading, science, and writing — in person and online.

✏️

School Supplies

Backpacks, notebooks, calculators, and books so no student starts the year without the basics.

🤝

Mentorship

Role models who help with study habits, goal-setting, and planning for high school, college, and careers.

💻

Digital Access

Help getting connected to devices and online learning resources that make modern schoolwork possible.
